One week in — what's next

You've been on Cordia for about a week. A few things people ask at this point:

  1. "I failed a block. Do I have to retake everything?"
    No. You can redo individual blocks. The new score replaces the old one.
  2. "What does the certificate actually mean?"
    It means you can demonstrate the rubric in a live environment. It's not a credential; it's a verifiable standard.
  3. "Should I buy a domain track now or after passing CordiaAIE?"
    After. The baseline is free, and the rubric carries directly into every domain track. Most people wait.
